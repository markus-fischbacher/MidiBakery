MidiBakery {version} is installed

  1. Restart Ableton Live.
  2. First time only: in Live's Settings > Link, Tempo & MIDI, choose MidiBakery as a Control Surface.
  3. The devices are in Live's browser: MidiBakery Chat (Max MIDI Effect), MidiBakery Reference Analyzer (Max Audio Effect), MidiBakery Generator and Transform (the clip view's Generate and Transform panels).

Each device's About button shows the versions in use.
